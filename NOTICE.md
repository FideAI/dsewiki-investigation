# Attribution and material boundaries

The MIT license covers original Fide code, writing, judgments and derived organization of the research. It does not relicense third-party reports, incident messages, benchmark material, quoted passages or linked sources. Limited source excerpts or identifiers in the research record remain attributable to their original sources. Full reports and raw incident/run archives are obtained separately from upstream.

Source links and acquisition scripts identify a fixed upstream commit. Preserve that attribution and the review-status limitations when reusing the analysis. Public release does not imply endorsement by OpenAI, the benchmark authors, the incident researchers or the systems assessed.
